# Surface brief: Ninth Coal (site: home, menu, reserve, visit)

Mode: Persuade (visitor decides to come and gets a seat). Fictional concept restaurant; all facts invented.
Audience and job: after-hours diners on phones; learn it is open, want the food, request a seat, find the door.
Action: request a seat (Reserve) and read the menu; hours and address always reachable.
Proof/content: authored fire and room illustration, full menu with prices, hours, walk-in policy, invented map. No reviews, awards or press.
Memorable moment: the lit window on the dark street that you step through; one live dithered fire.
Unresolved: none blocking.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the view from the dark street of the one lit window still open at 10pm, then the step inside. It refuses the dark-page-with-orange-glow restaurant default and the food-photo hero; light exists only where the fire is.
OWN-WORLD: Cold midnight street (blue-black #0B1014 family) against warm interior light. Interior ground is char brown-black; ash-white text tinted warm; gilt (flat gold leaf) for signwriter lettering; coal red for the fire and primary action; amber only inside the flame. Shopfront glass-lettering language: gilt caps with a dark outline on glass, arched window frames, hand-painted price boards, flat planes without outlines for silhouettes. Mix of a sturdy old-style display serif in caps and a warm humanist sans. Recognisable with content removed: arched lit aperture on cold dark.
STORY: Visitor sees it is open tonight, feels the room, scans the small menu, taps a seat request, finds the door. Believes it is serious cooking, not a bar snack menu.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height cold street. Centre-left, a tall arched window (about 55vw tall on desktop, near full width on mobile) with gilt glass-lettering NINTH COAL, FIRE KITCHEN, and hours; inside it the counter, the hearth and diner silhouettes as flat planes, the dithered live fire the brightest thing on the page. Open/closed status and a coal-red Reserve action sit below the window on the street. Left rail nav on desktop, slim bar plus bottom action bar on mobile.
FORM: Lit window seen from the street, position 5 of 7 on my ordered list (1 hearth glow on soot, 2 izakaya lantern street, 3 butcher chalk cut chart, 4 kitchen docket rail, 5 lit shopfront window, 6 matchbook, 7 night-bus timetable). Seed key 0ed71c0b, assigned index 5.
