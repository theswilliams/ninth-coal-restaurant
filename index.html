How the night burns

    The counter, the hearth, the grate.

    Everything in the room faces the fire. There is no other kitchen behind a door, so you watch your dinner get made.

    • The counter

      Twenty-four seats, all facing the fire. Half are held for people who walk in.

    • The hearth

      Oak, split and seasoned for two winters. It goes in at ten and is raked flat by eleven.

    • The grate

      Charcoal on the low side, open flame on the high. Steak goes where the heat suits it.

    On Mondays and Tuesdays the room is yours.

    We call them dark nights. The counter is closed, the hearth is lit early, and one long table seats up to twelve. One cook makes a set menu, start to finish, over the fire.
