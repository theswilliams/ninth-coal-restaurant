# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Vite + vanilla TypeScript, multi-page static (real separate HTML pages: Home, Menu, Reserve, Visit). Confirmed by user (2026-09-30). No framework; deployable to any static host. Build path: code-led (no image generation available in this harness).

## Users
Night-shift and after-hours diners (25–50): hospitality and theatre workers finishing their own shifts, couples and friend groups leaving a show or a bar who want a proper meal rather than a slice, travellers on the wrong clock. They browse on a phone, usually the same evening, and decide fast: is it open, what do I eat, can I get a seat.

## Product Purpose
Fictional restaurant **Ninth Coal**, a wood-fire kitchen open 10pm to 2am, counter seating around the fire. The site must make the place feel like somewhere you want to be at midnight, make the menu legible and appetising without photography, let a visitor request a seat in under a minute on a phone, and make hours, address and walk-in policy obvious. Concept portfolio project; nothing here is a real business.

## Positioning
Serious live-fire cooking for the hours when serious kitchens are closed. Small menu, one fire, everything cooked where you can watch it. The opposite of a late-night menu of deep-fried afterthoughts.

## Operating Context
Dark room, low light, phone in hand, often after an event. Walk-ins are first-come at the counter; a few tables can be held. Service is 10pm to 2am, Wednesday to Sunday; the kitchen takes last orders at 1:30am.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Fictional facts (concept only): 24 counter seats and 6 tables; open Wed–Sun 22:00–02:00; last orders 01:30; one wood-fired hearth and a charcoal grill; a menu of roughly 20 dishes in five groups (Snacks, From the embers, From the fire, Sides, Sweet); a wine and sake list; private dining for up to 12 after midnight on Monday and Tuesday ("the dark nights"). Address is an invented street in an unnamed city; the map is a drawn illustration, not a real map. The reservation form does not send anything; it confirms locally and says so. No photography exists; imagery is authored (SVG, CSS, canvas).

## Brand Commitments
Name: Ninth Coal. Voice: low, plain, warm; short sentences; no chef-worship, no "elevated". Must be presented publicly as a fictional concept project.

## Evidence on Hand
None real. All dishes, prices, hours, addresses and quotes are invented concept material and labelled as such. No awards, press, reviews or ratings are claimed.

## Product Principles
1. Atmosphere is information: the page should feel like the room at midnight before it explains anything.
2. Hours and the way in are never more than one glance away.
3. The menu is read like a menu: fast to scan, honest about price and allergens.
4. A seat request takes under a minute on a phone with one thumb.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on dark grounds (verify every muted text colour), full keyboard operation of menu filter, reservation form and navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion fallback that makes the fire static, and dietary markers that never rely on colour alone.
