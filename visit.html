Find the lit window

Nine Cinder Row. Down the steps, past the tram stop, toward the harbour. There is no sign; look for the only window still lit.

Hours

    Last orders at 01:30. Mondays and Tuesdays are kept for dark nights.

    Walking in

    Half the counter is held for people who simply turn up. Put your name on the slate by the door and we will find you a stool. After 23:30 the walk-in seats are counter only.

    A full room moves fast. On a busy Friday the wait is usually a drink at the bar.

    Getting in

    • Four steps down from Cinder Row. A ramp runs round the side from Quay Street; ring the bell.
    • Counter stools do not have backs. Ask for a chair at a table if you would rather.
    • The room is warm and a little loud near the hearth. Tables are quieter.
    • Service dogs are welcome.

    Before you come

    Do I need to book?

    No, but a booked seat is safe on a busy night. Half the counter is for walk-ins either way.

    Can we order a few dishes at a time?

    Please do. Food leaves the fire as it is ready, so order two or three and come back for more.

    Is there a vegetarian meal?

    Yes. The embers section is almost all vegetables, and most of the snacks and sides are vegetarian.

    What about children?

    Welcome at a table before 23:00. The counter is for adults after that.
